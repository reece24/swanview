Tamponade equalizes RA and wedge. Mixed pictures are common, so read the whole set together.

Read them right

  • Level and zero at the phlebostatic axis (4th intercostal space, mid-axillary line). 10 cm off shifts every number about 7.
  • End-expiration. On the ventilator, the low point before each breath. Breathing on their own, the high point before each breath.
  • Wedge with no more than 1.5 mL. Read at the a wave, then deflate. It should sit below PA diastolic.
  • Cardiac output with the balloon down: 10 mL at end-expiration, three readings within 10%.
  • SvO₂ drawn slowly from the distal port in the PA, balloon down.

Sources: EMCrit IBCC; LITFL; 2022 ESC/ERS pulmonary hypertension definitions (review); PAPi, Korabathina 2012.
